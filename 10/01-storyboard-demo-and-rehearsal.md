**Total planned spoken content:** approx. 6 minutes 10 seconds, plus a 45–75-second prototype demonstration (target ~7 minutes). This is a rehearsal plan, **not a timed delivery result**.

## Demonstration steps (45–75-second target; not tested in an audience)

1. Open the prototype's official start frame **S01** and briefly identify chosen game and edition.
2. Click **Continue**, show S02's **preset** card question; do not suggest this Figma prototype can accept free-form typed questions.
3. On **S03**, read the **DEMO / NOT VERIFIED** status, not a made-up authoritative ruling.
4. Click **Inspect source** → S04 and point to the sample-source warning and missing verified page.
5. Return to S03 or complete S04 → S05, explaining that successful navigation is **not** correctness of an official UNO rule.
6. If time permits, demonstrate **No supported evidence** and how its recovery avoids unsupported guesses.

## Backup if Figma prototype access fails

Open [Week 09 design](https://www.figma.com/design/shH7rwYFexBW16cpIfhWZy) and show the three Figma pages: 01 iterated prototype; 02 components; 03 S03 handoff. If the teacher cannot open the file, **do not** claim that the sharing setting was verified. Check permissions before class.

## Claims permitted versus not permitted

| Safe, verifiable claim | Unsupported claim to avoid |
| --- | --- |
| One anonymous person recalled the group searching and reading UNO rules | “Most players want this” or “the rule is officially correct” |
| Source-status wording and 12 component variants exist in Figma | “The AI now retrieves official rulebooks” |
| The main four clicks and four exception entries were structurally verified | “Three students completed the task successfully” |
| Designer review prompted source-status and recovery changes | “A/B testing proved completion time improved” |

## Reflection and one concrete next step

**Strength:** Source status is visible and the prototype exposes uncertainty instead of silently inventing citations.  
**Open uncertainty:** A player may still mistake the demo passage for genuine evidence.  
**Next action:** Recruit three consented testers; independently observe if they inspect S04 and correctly explain that the source is **not verified**. Afterward bind one permissioned real rulebook to its exact section/page and re-evaluate.
