## Presentation quality rules applied

- **One claim per slide**, with an action-oriented heading instead of a vague topic name.
- **Seven original SVG diagrams** using consistent navy/blue/white colors, concise on-slide words, readable labels and explicit alt text.
- **About five minutes**, planned as 20 + 35 + 40 + 45 + 85 + 45 + 30 seconds.
- **A selective one-minute Figma demo** of the evidence-inspection path, not a walkthrough of every Figma page.
- **A spoken English script** with short sentences for memorization, separate from slide text.
- **No fabricated participant claims**, invented official citations or overstated results.
- **A backup diagram** if the live prototype fails and direct links to the real Figma prototype.
