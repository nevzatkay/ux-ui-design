# Rulebook — Live-demo cues, timing and backup

## Rehearsal plan (5 minutes total)

| Slide | Target time | What you must communicate |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 01 Introduction | 20 seconds | Project name and presenter |
| 02 Problem | 35 seconds | Real +2/+4 dispute and online search |
| 03 Research | 40 seconds | One retrospective report; inference is not a measured usability result |
| 04 Design decision | 45 seconds | Ruleset → question → source status → inspect; clarification/abstention |
| 05 Prototype and demo | 85 seconds | Navigate selectively from S01 toward S04 and optionally S05 |
| 06 Iteration and verification | 45 seconds | Clearer demo warning and inspect action; technical rather than human validation |
| 07 Next step and thanks | 30 seconds | Three consented tests, real rulebook, one specific uncertainty |

**Total: 300 seconds.** Rehearse with a timer; the live clicking portion should be around 50–60 seconds. If Figma takes longer, cut the optional S05 click, not the honesty warning.

## Click plan — main route only

**Open in advance:** https://www.figma.com/proto/shH7rwYFexBW16cpIfhWZy?node-id=2-4&starting-point-node-id=2%3A4

1. **S01 Choose game & rules:** Show the selected game/edition and click Continue.
2. **S02 Ask rule:** Explain that the question is **pre-filled**, then click Ask.
3. **S03 Answer & source:** Read the **DEMO / NOT VERIFIED** label, then click Inspect source.
4. **S04 Read source:** Show the sample passage and warning. Do **not** claim it is a real verified rulebook citation.
5. **S05 Complete:** Optional if time allows. Emphasize that “review complete” means a navigation example, not official rule correctness.

**Never tour:** every 9th-week frame, all 12 component variants, or the full Figma library. The demonstration supports one slide claim.

## Backup if the prototype is slow or unavailable

The [slide 05 four-screen SVG](assets/05-demo-frames.svg) is a **schematic illustration** of the intended UI path, not a literal screenshot of the live Figma frames. Use it to narrate steps and describe what you would demonstrate; do not pretend the live app was executed.

The [editable Figma design](https://www.figma.com/design/shH7rwYFexBW16cpIfhWZy) is an alternative source to show a single screen if the prototype cannot start.

## Honesty checklist

- [x] One real anonymous retrospective UNO story (no invented quote or stopwatch result).
- [x] Source-status warning on the demo and explicitly fictional sample passage.
- [x] Actual documented technical Figma checks can be stated precisely.
- [ ] Three independent Week 08 user-test records (not yet collected).
- [ ] Official game rule linked with correct edition and passage.
- [ ] Instructor view access independently verified.
- [ ] Timed rehearsal recorded.

## Two short possible teacher questions

**Q: Why do you show the source, not just the answer?**  
A: “Players need to check that the answer comes from the correct rulebook, not just trust a summary.”

**Q: Did you test your design with real users?**  
A: “I have technical Figma checks, but the independent usability sessions are still pending. They are my next step.”
