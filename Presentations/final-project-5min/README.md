## Story and BookMD/PowerMD-oriented design

This short deck is based on the **presentation structure and visual narrative** guidance previously provided in the coursework: one clear claim per slide, followable story rather than a report, a purposeful visual, a small live demo, evidence separated from interpretations and a conclusion tied to one next step.

**Seven-slide arc:**

1. A disputed card creates a need.
2. One real rule disagreement led to online searching.
3. Research motivates showing the source itself.
4. Edition-aware, evidence-first task flow.
5. Selective live prototype demo, not a tour of every Figma frame.
6. Iteration and *genuine technical verification*, with explicit human-testing limitation.
7. One concrete next research step and closing thanks.

**Format:** Standard Markdown with `<!--@slide ...-->` markers and `---` delimiters, consistent with the repository's existing PowerMD decks. Seven original SVG visuals are stored under `assets/` and linked with relative Markdown paths.
