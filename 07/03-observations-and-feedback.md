## Evidence labeling rules

- **QUOTE:** Participant's exact words; if no words were said, write `No quote recorded`, not an invented sentence.
- **OBSERVATION:** What they actually did (button, backtrack, hesitation, reached screen), ideally in sequence.
- **INTERPRETATION:** Your provisional explanation; it is not equivalent to the observed behavior.
- **IMPROVEMENT:** A targeted change arising from the recorded problem.
