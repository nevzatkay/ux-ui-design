# Reflection — design-stage and post-test sections

## What was surprising in the **design walkthrough** (not participant feedback)

The main task needs an explicit source-check action to separate “an answer was displayed” from “the group can inspect evidence.” A click-through may look complete even though actual source retrieval and correct-edition checks are only simulated. This is a designer observation about the artifact, **not a finding from an outside student**.

## What was useful in the design walkthrough

The main route is short and traceable: choose rules → ask → read answer → open source → confirm. Making the missing-context, unsupported-edition and no-source states separately reachable exposes the system's refusal to invent a ruling.

## What I would change next (provisional)

Replace the placeholder excerpt with a verified, licensed source passage and clear page/section location; allow the player to correct the question and edition; test whether the “Read the rulebook source” action is discovered without hints. These priorities are hypotheses pending an actual student trial.
