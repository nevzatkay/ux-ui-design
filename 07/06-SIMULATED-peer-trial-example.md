## Possible reflection, not a tested conclusion

- **Surprise to investigate:** users might stop at the short answer instead of reading the source.
- **Useful behavior to look for:** whether the player voluntarily opens the passage and can state its status.
- **Proposed iteration:** make source status and inspect action a consistent, prominent pair.
- **Future success criterion:** an actual independent tester reaches S05 without verbal navigation hints, explains the source as sample-only and can recover from E02.
