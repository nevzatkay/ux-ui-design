# Consent and briefing script

## Read before each usability session

> Thank you for helping with a short student design test. I am testing the interface, not you. You will receive a short situation and will try the prototype without my guidance. Some features and rule text are only examples because this is an early version. I would like to write anonymous notes about what you do and what you choose to say; I will not record your voice, your screen, or personal details. You can skip a question or stop at any time without giving a reason. May I use these anonymous notes for my university assignment?

**Record for each participant:** consent **YES / NO**, date, anonymous ID. If NO, do not test or keep notes. No actual consent responses have been collected for Week 8 at the time of writing.

## Moderator behavior

- Hand over the prototype at S01. Read the scenario and task from `01-test-plan.md` without pointing to on-screen controls.
- Avoid leading comments such as “You should click Read source.”
- If asked “Is this right?”, reply “Please do what you would do if I were not here.”
- Do not claim the prototype's example UNO ruling is authoritative or that its cited page has been verified.
- Record direct participant speech exactly, with quotation marks; mark paraphrases as paraphrase.
- After testing, ask open prompts: “What was unclear?” and “What did you expect to happen?”
