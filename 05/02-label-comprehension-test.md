# 02 — Label-comprehension check (three students)

**Status: NOT CONDUCTED (0/3).** This is a ready-to-run protocol, not a claim of user evidence. It must be completed with real participants before calling the labels validated.

## Learning goal and method
Can a new player predict where to start, where to inspect the evidence, and how to recover if the rule is unavailable from the label language alone? Three short moderated, independent sessions (around 5 minutes) with people who have played a board or card game. No identifying information is required.

## Verbal consent and briefing
> I am testing navigation labels, not your ability. May I write anonymous notes about your responses? I will not record audio or names. You may stop at any time. Please say what you think a label means; there is no right answer you need to guess.

Record **consent before proceeding**. If consent is not given, do not include that person's responses.

## Task prompts — do not give away the target label
1. You are in a game and need to check a disputed move. Looking only at these navigation labels, where would you start? Why?
2. You see a short rule answer but want to verify it. Which label would you choose to see the supporting passage? What do you expect to find there?
3. If the system cannot find support in the chosen edition, which action would help you recover? Why?

**Labels to compare:** Choose game & rules; Ask a rule; Answer & source; Read source; Cannot verify; Change edition; Try again. Show identical options to each person without highlighting a preferred answer.
