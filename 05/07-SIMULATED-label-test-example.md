## Example analysis

- **Illustrative problem A:** "Ask a rule" might appear to be the initial step even if edition must be set first.
- **Illustrative hypothesis:** "1. Choose game & edition" would make the prerequisite clearer than "Choose game & rules".
- **Illustrative problem B:** "Answer & source" may be confused with "Read source".
- **Illustrative hypothesis:** Rename the detailed action "Open original rule passage" and distinguish the answer summary from the source-viewer screen.
- **Illustrative rejected alternative:** Showing only icons could save space but may reduce clarity and accessible labeling.
