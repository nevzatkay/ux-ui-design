## What is audited?

**Service:** [UltraBoardGames — UNO Game Rules](https://www.ultraboardgames.com/uno/game-rules.php).  
**Single task:** During a game, check whether the next player can put a Wild Draw Four (+4) on top of a Draw Two (+2).  
**Persona:** P01, an anonymized group player who described a past UNO rule dispute in Week 2.

## Deliverables

1. [Scope, task, evidence and method](audit-overview.md)
2. [Eight annotated findings](annotated-findings.md), including two accessibility concerns
3. [Task-state inventory and deceptive-pattern review](states-and-patterns.md)
4. [Updated persona and three risks for the Rulebook project](persona-and-project-risks.md)

Each finding identifies a **specific on-screen detail** rather than relying on a generic opinion. The evidence links identify the page and section, as screenshots of the live website were not available. A heading-level issue was inspected manually in the text-only page outline. The image alternative-text concern is provisional. No live keyboard, screen reader or DOM test was possible: **these findings are not a certification of WCAG compliance or failure**.

**Ethical limitation:** The source persona comes from one anonymized participant account. Added needs from the audit are explicitly *hypotheses*, not additional interview facts.
