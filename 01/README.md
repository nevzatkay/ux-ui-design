# Which rule applies at our table?

[Open Week 01–03 FigJam](https://www.figma.com/board/8CpfdHJFAHqpO9RcMVzzMj)

When a card changes a turn, board-game players need to decide their next move. The normal rule and the exception can appear in different rulebook sections, so the group pauses or proceeds without checking the right passage.

**Task question:** Can I still take my normal move? **Design goal:** Help a player select an appropriate ruleset and inspect the source behind a ruling or identify an unresolved situation.

## Hypothetical success criteria

Five players each receive four covered questions and one unresolved question. In at least 16 of 20 covered attempts, the player identifies a correct move and corresponding passage in under 90 seconds; in at least 4 of 5 unresolved attempts they recognize the missing support. **These are planned targets, not results.**
