# Which rule applies at our table?

[Open the Week 1 FigJam board](https://www.figma.com/board/8CpfdHJFAHqpO9RcMVzzMj)

When a card changes a turn, board-game players need to decide their next move, but the movement rule and the card exception can be in different booklets, so the group pauses or plays on with a guess.

The board follows one player's question, “Can I still take my normal move?” It maps the player's goal, the project goal, the edition constraint and the assumptions behind the idea. The riskiest assumption is that players will check a passage or acknowledge a gap while the table is waiting.

## Proposed success criteria

Five players each receive four questions covered by the selected rules and one unresolved question.

- In at least 16 of 20 covered attempts, the player states the correct move and points to the supporting passage within 90 seconds.
- In at least 4 of 5 unresolved attempts, the player identifies the gap before committing to a move.

These targets are for a future test. The scenario is illustrative.
