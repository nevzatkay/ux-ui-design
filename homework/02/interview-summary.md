# Anonymized interview summary — P01

**Participant background:** Person who participated in a group UNO game and had to resolve a specific special-card dispute. Text-based retrospective account; not a live observation. No personal or sensitive details were recorded.

## Question 1 — A concrete past rule dispute
**Prompt:** Tell me about a recent time your group needed to check a rule. What rule was unclear?

**Direct quote (original Turkish):**
> uno oynarken + kartları kullanma kurallarına baktık grupça +2 üstüne + 4 koyuluyor mu koyulmuyor mu diye

**Researcher translation:** During UNO, the group checked whether a +4 card can be played on a +2.

## Question 2 — What happened next?
**Prompt:** What did you do, where did you look, what did you find, and how did you decide?

**Direct quote (original Turkish):**
> internetten baktık ve koyulmucağına karar verdik kuralları okuduktan sonra

**Researcher translation:** The group searched online and, after reading the rules, decided that it could not be played.

## Answers and lessons
- **Reported behavior:** A specific card combination was disputed, the group searched online, read rules and reached a decision.
- **Interpretation:** Players may formulate rules questions as concrete actions; rule text contributed to the group's decision.
- **Design consequence:** Support questions about combinations of cards and show the supporting rules passage with the answer.

**Unknown:** Exact URL, official rulebook, edition, time taken, feelings, number of players, device and whether the ruling was correct for that edition. These are not invented. One example does not establish prevalence.
