## Research integrity

The only **primary user evidence** is one anonymized retrospective account (P01): the group disputed whether UNO +4 could follow +2, searched online, read rules, and reached a decision. Speed, emotion, exact source, edition and broader population needs are not evidenced. Proposed scores, impact and implementation feasibility are explicitly assumptions. The Week 3 external-service audit is **secondary design input**, not a participant quote or a usability test. Permission to quote the original interview is not confirmed in the available record; use the paraphrased anonymized summary here until consent is confirmed.

### Scope in one sentence

Help a group settle **one disputed UNO card combination** by finding an edition-scoped ruling with inspectable rule evidence or clearly stating when the available rules do not support an answer.
